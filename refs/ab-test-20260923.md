# 9-23 A/B Test: HyperQwen (new) vs Old Stack

## Setup

Both stacks served from the same 2× RTX 3080 20GB, TP=2, MTP, 224K context.
6 rounds per config, median reported.

| Stack | Image | Patches | MTP draft | Draft vocab |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **New (HyperQwen)** | syv-upg | 43 (int8 act, split-kv, unsorted sampler, etc.) | 4 | 0 (full 248K) |
| **Old (9-05)** | old | 9 (mtp, lm_head, embed, mamba fp16, etc.) | 2 | 0 (full 248K) |

## Results

| Task | New (median) | New (range) | Old (median) | Old (range) | Speedup |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Code gen | **100-103** | 92-110 | 56-61 | 50-65 | **~1.7×** |
| Chinese | **81** | 74-88 | 55 | 49-60 | **~1.5×** |
| Count | **175** | 150-200 | 130 | 110-145 | **~1.3×** |
| 12K ctx | **116** | 108-125 | 65 | 58-72 | **~1.8×** |
| 170K ctx | **84** | 78-92 | 45 | 40-52 | **~1.9×** |
| 215K ctx | **72** | 65-80 | 38 | 32-45 | **~1.9×** |
| 256K ctx (cold) | — | — | 34 | 28-38 | — |
| Prefill 22.5K | **1,261** | — | 901 | 820-980 | **~1.4×** |
| Prefill 51.5K | **1,276** | — | 1,141 | 1,050-1,200 | **~1.1×** |

## Why the new stack wins

1. **INT8 tensor core (4× GEMM rate)** — biggest single contributor, ~40%
2. **Split-KV Triton (23µs vs 57µs per layer)** — MTP verification, ~20%
3. **Unsorted sampler** — skips 140µs sort, ~5%
4. **MTP draft=4 vs 2** — more speculative tokens, ~10%
5. **FP8 KV + 0.86 gpu util** — slightly more KV space, ~3%

## What we learned

- **MTP draft=4 is the sweet spot** for this model. draft=6 showed no
  additional benefit (acceptance plateaus at ~50% for code).
- **Full 248K draft vocab is mandatory for Chinese.** The 40K draft
  (Danish+English) drops Chinese acceptance to 2%.
- **Long context degrades gracefully.** 215K ctx still does 72 tok/s
  (vs 100 at 0 ctx) — the SSM architecture helps here.
- **Prefix cache is essential for long documents.** Without it, round-2
  TTFT on a 25K token doc is 287-386s. With it, 2.5-5.3s (13×).
